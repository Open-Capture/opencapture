## 1.0.5

### en-US

- Firefox for Android: full-page and selected-area capture now work. The popup closes first, then the capture runs on the page underneath, also with "Desktop site" turned on.
- The popup, History and Account pages fit a phone screen, with larger buttons.
- The raw capture report no longer shows under the preview.
- Supporters keep the watermark tool when the account server can't be reached. It no longer asks you to buy it again.
- You stay signed in after restarting the browser.
- Credit and permission messages are now translated.
- Help links now go to Telegram and X.

### zh-CN

- Android 版 Firefox：现在可以截取整页和选定区域。弹出窗口会先关闭，再对下方的页面截图；开启“桌面版网站”时同样有效。
- 弹出窗口、历史记录和账户页面适配手机屏幕，按钮更大。
- 预览下方不再显示原始截图报告。
- 无法连接账户服务器时，支持者仍可使用水印工具，不会再被要求重新购买。
- 重启浏览器后仍保持登录。
- 积分和权限提示已翻译。
- 帮助链接改为指向 Telegram 和 X。
